# Upgrade notes: v4.3.1

What an operator does when upgrading an installation from v4.3.0 to v4.3.1.

## Accounts are found by their exact address

Sign-in, signup, verification resend and forgot password find an account by
its exact address (the account id), trimmed and without regard to case
([ECCdigital/tickets#259](https://github.com/ECCdigital/tickets/issues/259)).
Until v4.3.0 the address was a search pattern: `^own` signed in as
`owner@…` with the owner's password, and `.*` sent the reset link of the
first account. The same holds for every other lookup of an account by its
id, for example the session check of a token.

**Before the deploy**, check whether two accounts differ only in the case of
their address (`Erika@example.org` and `erika@example.org`). Of two such
accounts only one is found from v4.3.1 on - the one stored in lower case, if
there is one - so the person behind the other one is locked out of it. The check and
what to do with its findings are described in
[ECCdigital/tickets#283](https://github.com/ECCdigital/tickets/issues/283):
an account without bookings and without roles is deleted, otherwise the
installation stays on v4.3.0 until the accounts are resolved by hand.

A new signup stores the address trimmed and in lower case, as before for the
case.

## The sign-in and the registration tell no account

Change of the answers of `/auth`
([ECCdigital/tickets#259](https://github.com/ECCdigital/tickets/issues/259)),
for a client that reads them:

- `POST /auth/signin` answers an unknown account, a wrong password and an
  account that signs in with SSO alike: `401`
  `{ "message": "Invalid email or password" }` (was `404` `User not found`,
  `401` `Invalid password`, `401` `Invalid authentication type`). An
  unverified account answers `403` `User is not verified`, a suspended one
  `403` `User is suspended`, but only with the right password; with a wrong
  one they answer `401` like everyone else.
- `POST /auth/signup` and `POST /auth/resend-verification` answer `201`
  and `202` with the same body,
  `{ "success": true, "message": "If the address is not verified yet, a verification mail has been sent" }`
  (signup answered `201` without a body, the message of the resend was
  "If the address belongs to an unverified account, …").
- Signup, verification resend and forgot password answer before the account
  is looked up and before the mail goes out. A mail that fails is logged by
  the backend (`after-answer.js`, `… failed`), never answered: signup no
  longer answers `500` with the text of the mail server, forgot password no
  longer `500`. The person can request the mail again.

The Admin UI reads `403` `User is not verified` to offer the verification
mail again, as before; it needs no newer version for this change.
